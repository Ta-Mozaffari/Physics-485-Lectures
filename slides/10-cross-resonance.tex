\begin{frame}{Cross-resonance gates in superconducting circuits}
  \small
  \begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
    \column{0.48\textwidth}
    \textbf{Drive the control at the target's frequency}

    Two coupled transmons let a microwave drive on the control influence the
    target. The drive is tuned near the target transition frequency,
    $\omega_d\approx\omega_t$.

    \centering
    \begin{tikzpicture}[x=0.9cm,y=0.78cm,>=Stealth]
      \draw[thick,fill=blue!12,rounded corners=2pt] (-2.4,-0.25) rectangle (-0.55,1.0);
      \draw[thick,fill=orange!18,rounded corners=2pt] (0.55,-0.25) rectangle (2.4,1.0);
      \node[align=center] at (-1.48,0.38) {control\\transmon\\$\omega_c$};
      \node[align=center] at (1.48,0.38) {target\\transmon\\$\omega_t$};
      \draw[thick] (-0.55,0.38)--(-0.16,0.38);
      \draw[thick] (-0.16,0.06)--(-0.16,0.70);
      \draw[thick] (0.16,0.06)--(0.16,0.70);
      \draw[thick] (0.16,0.38)--(0.55,0.38);
      \node[below] at (0,-0.25) {weak coupling};
      \draw[->,very thick,red!75!black] (-1.48,1.75)--(-1.48,1.05)
        node[midway,right,align=left] {microwave drive\\$\omega_d\approx\omega_t$};
    \end{tikzpicture}

    \column{0.48\textwidth}
    \centering
    \textbf{Conditional Rabi rotation of the target}

    \begin{tikzpicture}[x=0.92cm,y=0.86cm,>=Stealth]
      \foreach \cx/\sgn/\lab in {-1.55/1/0,1.55/-1/1} {
        \draw[gray!55] (\cx,0) circle (0.92);
        \draw[->,gray] (\cx-1.05,0)--(\cx+1.08,0) node[right] {$y$};
        \draw[->,gray] (\cx,-1.08)--(\cx,1.12) node[above] {$z$};
        \draw[very thick,blue!75!black] (\cx,0)--(\cx,0.78)
          node[above] {$|0\rangle_t$};
        \draw[->,very thick,red!75!black] (\cx,0.78)
          arc[start angle=90,end angle={90-55*\sgn},radius=0.78];
        \draw[very thick,red!75!black] (\cx,0)--({\cx+0.78*sin(55*\sgn)},{0.78*cos(55*\sgn)});
        \node[below] at (\cx,-1.12) {control $|\lab\rangle_c$};
        \node[red!75!black] at ({\cx+0.52*\sgn},0.48) {$\theta$};
      }
    \end{tikzpicture}

    \raggedright
    The control state sets the direction of the target's rotation. As the pulse
    continues, the rotation angle grows: this is a conditional Rabi oscillation.
  \end{columns}

  \vspace{0.9em}
  \centering
  \small
  Effective interaction: $H_{\mathrm{CR}}\sim Z_cX_t$. Echo pulses and
  single-qubit rotations turn the calibrated interaction into a CNOT gate.
\end{frame}
